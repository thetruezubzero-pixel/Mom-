SOLAR EXPLORER

Free-roam a physically-scaled model of the solar system, no objectives or timer — just fly. Real elliptical orbits (Kepler's laws), real axial tilts, real major moons, and a backdrop of the actual brightest stars at their real positions — inner worlds visibly orbit, outer worlds barely creep, just like reality. Use Quick Travel to jump straight to any planet, and open EARTH OPS near Earth for a real-geography air/sea/land/satellite logistics overlay.
